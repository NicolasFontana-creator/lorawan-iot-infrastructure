# Results and scope

[Español](README.md) · [English](README.en.md)

The report confirms functional integration of the software tools. Screenshots record the state of the trial. No latency, availability or energy measurements were reported. RStudio, predictive analysis and improved handling of large data volumes are future work.

Pages 4–15 document the views, queries and reporting workflow. The conclusion on p. 16 concerns software integration. The panels are historical evidence and provide neither current service status nor radio coverage measurements. Reproduction requires the Node-RED flows, dashboard exports, queries and a sanitized LoRaWAN access configuration.

Source: Nicolás Nahuel Fontana, *Gestión eficiente de recursos en ciudades inteligentes. Desarrollo de una plataforma IoT*, Telecommunications Laboratory, UTN FR Paraná.
